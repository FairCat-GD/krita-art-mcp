# Part 9.1: plugin core for paint.stroke

- **Built:**
  - **The contract's plugin files.**
    - `port.DocumentsPort` gains `set_brush`, `brush`, `paint_ability`, `paint_path`,
      `cumulative_undo`, and, for the harness, `view_state`, `set_view`,
      `brush_state(document, preset_ids)` and `set_brush_state`. The signatures match the
      adapter's.
    - `ledger.Step.witness` is `(layer_id, rect, sha_before, sha_after)` (the `Witness` alias).
    - `history.move` passes the witness side to `_shows`, which reads `port.layer_rects` over
      the rect only after the label and the node state match. The hash must be `after` before an
      undo and `before` after it, and the reverse for a redo. A mismatch is the wrong-label
      outcome, and `known` never skips the pixel read.
    - `history.LABELS["paint.stroke"]` is "Scripted Brush Stroke". Steps without a witness
      behave exactly as before: the existing 442 plugin tests pass unchanged.
  - **`bridge_core/brushes.py`** (pure):
    - `brush_reach(xml)` returns `{engine, tip, spacing, scatter, reasons}`:
      - the engine is `paintopid`;
      - the tip comes from the `brush_definition` param's own XML: an `auto_brush` with a
        `circle` mask is round, with a `rect` mask square, any other type an image;
      - spacing is the `Brush` element's `spacing`;
      - scatter is `ScatterValue` when `PressureScatter` is on and an axis is on, 0 when it is
        off. It is unknown when a key is missing, a flag is not true or false, or the strength
        lies outside 0–5.
    - `reasons` lists `brush_engine` (not `paintbrush`), `brush_tip` and `brush_scatter`.
    - `margin_px(size, tip, scatter)` is `max(32, size × (e + s))`, with e 0.5 for round and
      √2⁄2 for square and image.
  - **`bridge_core/painting.py`:** `handlers(registry, port, resources, data)` gives
    `paint.stroke`, guarded like `layer.update`.
    - **The arguments** are exact. `eraser` with `alpha_lock` is `INVALID_ARGUMENT`, and so is
      a colour alpha other than 1.
    - **The refusals come in the brief's order.** The protected target is `LOCKED_TARGET` before
      the type and space checks. A selection-mask child is `LAYER_HAS_LOCAL_SELECTION`, with its
      ids in `mask_ids`.
    - **The preset:**
      - an unknown preset is refused with the 5 nearest file ids (`difflib`, cutoff 0);
      - the listed fingerprint is checked against `preset_sha256`;
      - a preset whose XML Krita cannot write is `INVALID_ARGUMENT`, because the result's
        `preset_sha256` must be a string.
    - **Inside `in_front`:** the brush is set, then checked. Preset, blend, eraser and alpha lock
      must be taken; other fields are reported as Krita holds them. Then `paint_ability`, then the
      reach from the effective XML (region refuses an unnamed reach with `reasons`), then R and
      R′.
    - **R and R′:**
      - R is the control-point bounds, grown with floor and ceil, then clipped.
      - R′ grows the unclipped R by `max(32, effective size)`.
      - The target is read over R′ (region), or over the clipped union of its old bounds and R′
        (full).
      - Each protected paint layer meeting the scope is hashed over its bounds ∩ scope.
    - **Before painting:**
      - both the scope and the target rect pass `refuse_unverifiable`;
      - `OUTSIDE_ALLOWED_MASK` is judged over R in either mode;
      - a refresh, the before-read, `brush()` compared whole (its XML included);
      - then the fingerprint, `before_change()` and `paint_path`.
    - **After the stroke:**
      - the after-read keeps the first changed protected layer's buffer;
      - `check` runs on the composite;
      - D comes from `verification.changes` on the target;
      - the step is pushed with its witness, or the ledger is sealed when D is empty.
    - **The tree is checked** for protected bounds. In region scope only, the ring holds D ⊆ R,
      and the target's bounds stay inside the bounding box of its old bounds and the unclipped
      R′. Full scope rolls back no stroke for its reach. When the target's new bounds pass the
      read rect, `changed_bounds_px` widens to include them; the witness stays D.
    - **An unnamed reach in full scope:** an unknown tip counts as √2⁄2 and an unknown scatter as
      5, so R only widens. `reach.tip` is `"unknown"`, and `reach.scatter` is the value used.
    - **The step's region** is the composite's changed bounds ∪ D.
    - **Rollback.** On the ledger: one `move(..., known=step.after)` and `rolled_back()`.
      Otherwise:
      - seal, refresh, re-read;
      - unchanged: confirmed with no undo (`rolled_back: false, rollback_verified: true`);
      - changed with a target diff: one `move` of an unnamed step whose witness is built from
        the re-read;
      - anything else: unsafe.
      - After an undo: a refresh, `again`, `same()`, and the target's bounds equal to its old
        bounds.
      - The kept protected buffer is counted against a fresh read of that layer. After-buffers
        are dropped first.
      - A `BaseException` after `before_change()` seals, marks the document unsafe and
        re-raises.
    - **The failure details** carry `protected_layer_ids`, `protected_pixels_changed` and
      `target_outside_reach` on every rollback error, not only `PROTECTED_REGION_CHANGED`.
  - **`verification`:**
    - `Reading` keeps `target_id`, per-layer `rects` and `kept`.
    - `read(..., rects, expected)` reads each layer over its own rect. An empty scope still
      reads layers that have rects.
    - `again()` repeats a reading.
    - `changes(old, new, rect)` returns the changed-pixel count and bounds, in bands, with no
      per-pixel loop.
    - `crop(buffer, outer, inner)` takes rows from a memoryview.
  - **The harness** (harness folder only, no lease):
    - `harness.view` (null reads; the document must be in front; zoom > 0, finite rotation,
      integer pan);
    - `harness.brush_state` read/write, by preset file id, passed straight to the port (the
      brush is window-wide, so no view comes forward);
    - `harness.undo`: Krita's Undo in `in_front`, with `touched()` and no seal.
  - **`Jobs.retention()`** and the `probe.retention` op, which the `Bridge` registers beside
    `server_info`.
  - **Capabilities:** `stroke.native_brush`, `stroke.smoothing` and `selection.enforced` are
    supported since 0.9.0.
  - **The fake** (`tests/fixtures/fake_krita.py`):
    - canvas-sized BGRA `buffers` per layer, which `paintable(key)` makes;
    - `paint_path` writes a dab box (radius size/2) at each on-curve point, plus `stray`
      rects, into the target and the composite (an eraser leaves the composite alone), grows the
      unclipped bounds, runs `on_paint` hooks, pushes "Scripted Brush Stroke", and fails via
      `fail["paint_path"]` (before) or `fail["paint_path_after"]` (after);
    - undo snapshots carry the buffers and the composite;
    - brush state (`ignore_brush`, `adjust`, `brush_drift`, `brush_order`), `ability`,
      `cumulative`, view state, and preset state by file id;
    - `PRESETS`, `preset_xml()` and `FakeResources.add_stroke_presets()`.
    - The default `FakeResources` keeps its three presets, so server tests are unchanged.
      `PRESET_XML` (Basic Round) is now a round auto brush with its scatter off, so a region
      stroke with it works in the server's `running_world`.
  - **`tests/support/worlds.py`** registers `painting.handlers`.
  - **`tests/plugin/test_painting.py`**, plus one test each in `test_verification.py`,
    `test_bridge_core.py` and `test_resources.py`. The last had its reordered-XML fixture
    rebuilt from the new `PRESET_XML`.

- **Numbers:** 554 plugin-core tests (+112 from 442), 436 server tests (±0 from 436).
- **For the shared documents:**
  - **CHANGELOG:**
    - "`paint.stroke` in the plugin: one native Krita brush stroke per call, checked in its own
      job against protection and undone by native undo on a leak."
    - "History tells strokes apart by their pixels: a hand undo or another stroke on top stops
      `krita_history_step`."
    - "`krita_server_info` reports `stroke.native_brush`, `stroke.smoothing` and
      `selection.enforced` as supported since 0.9.0."
  - **Deliberate decisions:**
    - every rollback error carries the protected and reach fields;
    - an unwitnessable change (no target diff) is unsafe;
    - nothing changed after a raise is `rolled_back: false, rollback_verified: true`;
    - `target_outside_reach` failures are `PROTECTED_REGION_CHANGED`, in region scope only;
    - `OUTSIDE_ALLOWED_MASK` is judged over R even in full mode;
    - a preset with no fingerprint is `INVALID_ARGUMENT`;
    - the target-bounds check uses the bounding box of the old bounds and R′.
  - **Backlog:** none.

    `changed_bounds_px` widens to the target's new bounds when they pass the read rect.
  - **`harness.brush_state`:** keyed by preset file id. The port's parameter is `preset_ids`, and
