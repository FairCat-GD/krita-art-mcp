Session X2 of round 2, 2026-11-12. The code and tests of every session so far (R1, R2, R3, D1, F1,
C1, M1, X1a, E1, L1, B1, T1 and S1) were read cold against `FEATURE-recovery-and-construction.md`

**Method.** The product was read twice, by six independent readers in two waves, each wave split
three ways: the verified-mutation frame and the construction ops on it (`mutation`, `history`,
`ledger`, `nodes`, `verification`, `protection`, `layers`, `translations`, `transforms`, `affine`,
`layer_update`, the adapter's `documents`, the fake and their tests); the change markers, the
outcome rule, retries, the save's finisher, the document branch and tapers (`jobs`, `registry`,
`saving`, `branching`, `opening`, `dynamics`, `painting`, the server's `dispatch`, `operations`,
`saving`, `branching`, the journal and the client, and their tests); and the contract (the five new
schemas and the changed ones, the hints, the capability matrix, the instructions, parity,
`amend_schemas`, the probe's new modules and every document). Every reader read its files in full
and proved what it claimed by running it in memory: on the fake, `tests/plugin`'s worlds,
`tests/support/worlds.py` and the Codex renderer port. The second wave read without sight of the
first wave's notes, with two exceptions said here so they are not mistaken for independence: the
second outcomes brief added three questions drawn from the first wave (a later stroke after a
failed taper, the ids a failure's details name, the branch document's own file), and a first
second-wave frame brief that leaked the first wave's questions was stopped and relaunched with the
first wave's brief verbatim. All three second-wave readers were interrupted and resumed from where
they stopped. Each finding was then verified on the code path by this session before it was kept;
F1's three consequences were reproduced here on the fake. `verify`, `doctor` and `probe` ran as
found, with the owner's go-ahead, before anything else (§7). Every claim points at `file:line` on
commit `ac27e19` or at a measurement.

**Severity.** *High*: an invariant is weakened, or a planned session is blocked as written.
*Medium*: a defect or debt that will cost a later session. *Low*: drift or untidiness. *Info*:
recorded, no remedy.

## 3. Findings

### High

**F1. L1's four layer ops and `layer_update` refuse, scope and judge from a tree read before Krita
is idle.** `layers.create`, `duplicate`, `reorder` and `remove` read `port.tree()` first
(`bridge_core/layers.py:30,126,197,280`), and `layer_update.update` likewise
(`layer_update.py:29`). The adapter's `tree()` never waits (`krita_adapter/documents.py:117-126`).
The refusals run on that tree, the read scope is computed from its bounds (`layers.py:41,59,137,305`),
and the same tree goes to `Mutation.run(tree=...)` as the start state the rollback confirms against
(`layers.py:175,275,337`; `layer_update.py:93`; `mutation.py:229`). S1's review fixed exactly this
for translate and the affine ops (`transforms.py:88-93`, `affine.py:131-137`: re-read once the
before-read has waited, refuse retryably if anything watched moved), and not here. Found by both
frame reads. *Run,* on `tests/plugin/test_layers.py`'s world, a change landing at the first
refresh:
- **A protected layer removed.** Protected LOCKED moves into GROUP; `layer.remove` of GROUP passes
  `refuse_held` on the stale tree, removes both, and its judge refuses. The rollback's undo is
  right, but the after-check compares with the stale start: moves `['undo', 'redo']`,
  `ROLLBACK_FAILED`, `rolled_back: false`, unsafe, LOCKED gone.
- **K53's crash path.** A transform mask lands on LEFT during `layer.duplicate` of LEFT;
  `_refuse_transform_mask` read the stale tree, so the copy holds a copied transform mask, the judge
  refuses, and the rollback undoes "Add Layer", the attach of that copy: the step that crashed Krita
  4 times of 4 (K53). The fake records the undo and ends unsafe.
- **A leak verified.** An allowed mask of columns 0–2; LEFT's bounds grow from 3 to 5 wide at the
  wait; `layer.remove` of LEFT in region scope reads over the stale bounds and completes with
  `changed_px: 7`, `changed_outside_px: 0`, though columns 3 and 4, outside the allowed mask,
  changed. In full scope it is refused `PROTECTED_REGION_CHANGED`.
*Cost:* needs a change in flight when the call starts, the owner editing that document, which is the
premise S1's review accepted. Then invariants 4 and 5 both thin: a protected layer can be removed,
a correct undo reversed, a leak verified, and one path leads to a Krita crash with the owner's
documents open. The stroke and fill read their refusals and scope the same way (`painting.py:200`),
though their start state is read inside `run`. *Remedy:* the S1 pattern in each op: the before-read
first (a paint-layer create, which has none, refreshes first), then the tree read again, a retryable
refusal if anything watched or a scoped bound moved, and the waited tree passed as `before` and
`tree=`; or a wait at the top of `tree()`. Tests with a change landing at the first wait, one per
op.

### Low
